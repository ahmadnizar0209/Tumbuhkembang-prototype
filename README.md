# TumbuhKembang — interactive prototype (Stage 7, v1.6)

Offline HTML prototype of TumbuhKembang, an ABA data-collection app for behaviour therapists (iPhone and iPad).

**Open it:** https://ahmadnizar0209.github.io/Tumbuhkembang-prototype/

- Switch device (iPhone SE, iPhone 16, iPad) and Gratis / Versi Penuh at the top.
- v1.1 (19 Nov 2026): interval targets now have a per-target observation window (D-13) — in a live session, tap "Mulai pengamatan" on an interval card to start its interval timing.
- v1.2 (19 Nov 2026): the ABC sheet lets you type your own anteseden and konsekuensi (D-14); they are saved for that client and offered again next session. "Lainnya" has been removed from both lists.
- v1.3 (19 Nov 2026): the prompt hierarchy in Ubah Program is a checklist (D-15) — tick only the levels a programme uses; the live session card shows just those.
- v1.3.1 (19 Nov 2026): fix — on iPad the banner ad was rendered outside the visible detail pane on Pengaturan, so it never appeared.
- v1.3.2 (19 Nov 2026): wording — the Jadwal week view is now labelled "Pekan" instead of "Minggu", which also means Sunday.
- v1.6 (19 Nov 2026): the client screen gains "Jadwalkan Sesi" (D-18) — the appointment form opens with that client already set. Saving any appointment now warns if it clashes with another client.
- v1.5 (19 Nov 2026): "Mulai Sesi" on the client screen now checks the calendar first (D-17) — it uses a schedule within 15 minutes either side of now, otherwise it offers an incidental 60-minute schedule or starting with none, and warns about clashes with other clients.
- v1.4 (19 Nov 2026): appointments gain "Jadwalkan Ulang" (D-16) — available until an hour before the start; the old slot stays visible as "Dijadwalkan ulang" with the new date and time. "Tidak Hadir" is now active only from 15 minutes after the start time, with no session running.
- All clients and data are made-up demo data. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- The link is case-sensitive: keep the capital T.
- On a phone, open the link in Safari and turn the phone sideways for a larger view.
